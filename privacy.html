Privacy Policy

How Asteroid Tech handles information in its apps.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

On this page

This Privacy Policy explains how Asteroid Tech (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use our Android apps published on Google Play (the “Apps”), including Phone Control Voice Assistant, and this website.

BY INSTALLING OR USING OUR APPS, YOU AGREE TO THIS PRIVACY POLICY. IF YOU DO NOT AGREE, PLEASE DO NOT USE THE APPS.

01Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, email or phone number inside the Apps. Depending on how you use the Apps, the following information may be processed:

  • Voice input: when you tap the microphone, your speech is converted to text using your device's speech recognition service (for example Google speech services) so the App can understand your command.
  • Commands and queries: the text of your request may be sent to the service needed to answer it (for example a web search, Wikipedia, a weather or news provider).
  • Device and usage information: app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, device model, Android version and advertising ID.
  • Approximate location: may be used by advertising partners and, if you allow it, to give local results such as the weather.

02App Permissions

Our Apps only request the permissions needed for their features. For example:

  • Microphone — to listen to your voice commands after you tap the mic button. We do not record in the background.
  • Internet — to search the web, get weather and news, and show ads.
  • Other permissions (such as calendar or location) are requested only when you use a feature that needs them, and you can refuse or revoke them in your Android settings at any time.

03How We Use Information

  • to run the Apps and respond to your voice commands;
  • to fix bugs, measure performance and improve our Apps;
  • to show ads that keep the Apps free;
  • to answer you when you contact our support.

04Advertising (AdMob)

Our Apps display ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your advertising ID, device information, IP address and approximate location to show ads and measure their performance. In some regions you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings (Settings › Google › Ads). Learn more in the Google ads policy and Google Privacy Policy.

05Third-Party Services

We use services from third parties that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (if enabled in the App)

When you ask the App to search or open a website, that service receives your query and handles it under its own policy.

06Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed above to operate the Apps and show ads, when required by law, or as part of a merger or sale of our business.

07Data Security

Data sent by the Apps is encrypted in transit. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

08Retention & Your Choices

We do not keep a history of your voice commands on our own servers. Information held by third-party services is kept according to their policies.

You can revoke permissions, reset your advertising ID, or uninstall the App at any time. For any privacy request, email hightechandroid@outlook.com.

09Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Apps after an update means you accept the changes.

11Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy? Email support@astrd.online or use our Contact page. Asteroid Tech — Morocco.
