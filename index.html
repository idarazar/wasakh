Got A Question?

Is Phone Control Voice Assistant free?
Yes. The app is free to download on Google Play and is supported by ads.
What can I do with my voice?
You can search the web and Wikipedia, check the weather, follow the news, play music, check your calendar, ask for suggestions and control compatible smart-home features. Just tap the microphone and speak.
Why does the app need microphone access?
The microphone is used only to hear the commands you speak after you tap the mic button. See our Privacy Policy for details.
When are the new apps coming?
We are working on new voice-powered apps. Follow us on Google Play to be the first to know when they launch.
